# Changelog

## v1.1.0 — 2026-10-15

### New
- Meanings now read like everyday English, with short example sentences
- "Similar" and "Opposite" words under every meaning; tap one to look it up, then press back to return
- See how a word is pronounced, written out
- Meanings start loading while you're still aiming, so they show up faster
- Choose Light, Dark or Match phone from the new ⋮ menu
- Switch online meanings off any time; the built-in dictionary still works with no internet

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-14

### New
- Point your phone at a book and rest the lens on a word: a highlighter sweeps across it and the meaning slides up in under a second
- Tap any word on screen to look it up straight away
- A built-in dictionary with 117,000 meanings that works with no internet, most common meaning first, with examples and similar words
- Understands word forms: "ran" shows "run", "children" shows "child"
- Words split across two lines with a hyphen are joined back together
- Shows the sentence the word came from, so you know it read the right one
- Hear any word said out loud
- Reading light for dim rooms, pinch to zoom, and the screen stays awake while you read
